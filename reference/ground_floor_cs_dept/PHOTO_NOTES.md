# Ground floor (CS department) — what the photos show

Written from the team's photos (uploaded 2026-10-12/13). Use this as the source of truth when building the interior in Unity or Blender. Colours are described as they look in the photos (the photos are dim, so the real surfaces are a little lighter).

## Corridors and entrance
Photos: `corridor_1_office_and_principal_doors`, `corridor_2_notice_board`, `corridor_3_faculty_room_to_end`, `main_door_entrance_hall`, `detailed photo` (`groundfloor.jpeg`).
- **Corridor:** narrow (about 2.5 to 3 m), long, runs to a window with a black frame at the far end and a water cooler. Ceiling off-white / pale grey with **square white LED panels**; a lower beam crosses the ceiling at intervals. Walls **pale pink** with a **dark granite skirting** (about 0.3 m). Floor is **polished dark red-brown granite** with **black inlay stripes** parallel to the walls. Cables and white conduits run along the upper walls.
- **Doors:** dark brown wood with a **glass transom** above and a sign above (blue plate, white text, e.g. `ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE G2`, `PRINCIPAL G1`). A blue hanging bracket sign reads `PRINCIPAL / Dr. PREETHA MATHEW K`.
- **Notice boards:** a wooden-framed glass case, three panels, titled `PLACEMENT & TRAINING NOTICE BOARD`; also blue pin boards with papers beside doors.
- **EXIT:** a green plate with `EXIT`, hanging from the ceiling toward the end of the corridor.
- **Entrance hall (main door):** the walls are a much stronger **coral / salmon pink** than the corridor. Black-framed **glass double doors** with a glass transom, notice boards on both side walls, dark granite skirting, same polished granite floor. Straight ahead is a brown double door, `ELECTRICAL PANEL ROOM G3`.

## Principal's room (G1)
Photos: `principal_room_inside_*`, `principal_room_ceiling`, `principles_room` (door).
- **Ceiling:** painted **pale blue-grey / lavender**, not white. **Cream-bordered fluorescent fixtures**, a **square LED panel**, three **cream ceiling fans**.
- **Walls:** **cream** (pale, slightly pink). Floor: **pale grey-cream tiles**.
- **Layout:** the desk (red top) sits against the far wall under a window with **orange-brown zebra blinds**; a **white split AC** above it. **Rows of wine-red cushioned wooden armchairs** face the desk (about 3 rows of 4). On the left: a **red curtain with cream trim**, a **wooden-framed trophy cabinet** and a **black steel cupboard**, a clock and a wall projector. On the side walls: a **dark wooden board `LIST OF PRINCIPALS, CUCEK`** with white text, **two floral armchairs and a floral sofa** with a glass-top coffee table, calendars, a small grey cabinet, and a **brown back door with an exhaust fan** above it. A wall-mounted network cabinet and a PC sit at the right.

## Administrative office (G2) and Exam Cell (one big room, two sections, two doors)
Photos: `office_*`, `exam_cell_door_and_corridor`, `exam_cell.jpeg` (older).
- It is **one large open-plan room**. The **Office** door (brown, glass transom, `ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICE G2`) and the **Exam Cell** door (dark grey-brown steel door, signs `OFFICE` and `EXAM CELL`) both open into it; it is the same space, split into two sections.
- **Ceiling:** painted **grey-lilac / blue-grey** with fans and fluorescent tubes; a deep **beam** crosses the room. **Walls:** peach-pink on one side, **yellow-cream** at the far end. **Floor:** cream/beige tiles.
- **Windows:** three large **black-framed windows with grilles** on the outer wall.
- **Furniture:** tall **dark-brown reception counters** with grey stained tops near the doors, many **desks with computers**, **wooden cane-back chairs**, red office chairs, a **row of olive-green steel cupboards** along the back wall, a **whiteboard** (fee details), a plant, a water can, notice papers.

## Toilets (gents and ladies)
Photos: `toilets_front_view_gents_and_ladies`, `toilet_inside_1`, `_2`, `_3_urinals`, `toilet.jpeg`, `toilet_side_corridor`.
- **Entrance:** two doors beside a **square pillar** (cream, with granite base). Door to the left: dark brown wooden door in a brown wooden frame with a paper sign. `LADIES TOILET` blue plate over the right opening.
- **Inside:** walls are **tiled** — a **medium blue-violet band** from the floor to about 0.9 m, then **pale blue-grey / white glossy tiles** up to about 2.2 m, then peach plaster above. **Floor: light blue tiles** (darker blue in the wet area by the basins). Pipes in grey along the walls and ceiling.
- **Fittings:** two **white wall-hung wash basins** with taps; **stalls** with **cream / off-white doors** in tiled partitions; **three white urinals with dark dividers** (gents); orange plastic buckets; a **black-framed barred window**.

## Classrooms (see also the earlier classroom photos)
Dark wood desks with a front panel and attached back-rest benches, cream tile floor, red curtains on a barred window, whiteboard on the side wall, a low wooden platform with a plank desk and a slatted armchair at the front.
